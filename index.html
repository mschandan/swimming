Instructions & Guidelines for Swimmers

General Guidelines

  • No entry without swimming pool ID card.
  • Swimmers must enter details in the register at the time of entry and exit.
  • Swimmers are only allowed during their allotted/booked slot.
  • Leave pool 5 minutes before slot completion for shower & exit preparations.
  • Children below 12 years must be accompanied by parents.
  • Do not bring expensive personal articles to the pool.

Health & Hygiene

  • Swimming costume is mandatory for all swimmers.
  • Remove shoes outside the swimming pool premises.
  • Swimmers with long hair must wear a swimming cap.
  • Persons suffering from epilepsy, open wounds, skin/eye infections, or communicable diseases are strictly prohibited.
  • Take a shower before entering and after exiting the pool.
  • Smoking, intoxicants, and food items are strictly forbidden.
  • Do not apply oil or ointment on the body prior to swimming.

Safety Protocols

  • Swimming is strictly prohibited without a Coach and Lifeguard present.
  • Beginners must remain at the shallow end.
  • Deck area & changing room floors are slippery—walk carefully and do not run.
  • Cut nails regularly; do not wear wrist/neck threads, bands, or metallic objects (rings, watches, bangles).
  • Diving and horse-play/pushing are strictly forbidden.
  • Photography is prohibited without explicit permission from FIC/Coach.
  • Rule violations will result in immediate cancellation of membership.

Note: The Coach may reserve any swimming slot for Inter-IIT Practice purposes.
